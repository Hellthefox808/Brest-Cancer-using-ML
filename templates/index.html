Clinical Background

The Wisconsin Breast Cancer Database was collected by Dr. William H. Wolberg at the University of Wisconsin Hospitals. Fine Needle Aspiration (FNA) samples are mapped across 9 morphological dimensions.

Diagnostic Classes

Class 2 (Benign): Healthy, non-cancerous cellular patterns that do not invade adjacent tissues.
Class 4 (Malignant): Aggressive, abnormal cell division requiring urgent medical oncology review.
